Is Bistro creating or destroying value for Eternal?

A switching value analysis of self cannibalisation in quick commerce
Six slide board deck  ·  ECO507 Managerial Economics and Business Analysis  ·  Data through Q1FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026)
Sources: 12 Eternal Ltd shareholders' letters and Swiggy Ltd's Q1FY27 letter, filed with BSE. Independent analysis of public filings. Not investment advice.
Slide 1 · Executive summary

Cannibalisation was never the risk. Ask about the margin, and about the capital.

Recommendation. Do not price Bistro as a threat to Zomato. Migrating a rupee of order value from the marketplace to an owned kitchen is accretive as long as Bistro clears 10.2% contribution on its own order value, and Bistro is currently too small to move the group either way. Monitor the margin, not the cannibalisation rate.

10.2%
Contribution Bistro must earn on its own order value. It is the marketplace's own disclosed margin. At 60% cannibalisation the bar falls to 6.1%.

Year on year, Q1FY26 to Q1FY27. It accelerated straight through the Bistro scale up, while contribution margin rose from 9.9% to 10.2%.

Even at total cannibalisation with zero Bistro margin, the contribution Eternal gives up is about ₹10 crore a quarter against ₹1,097 crore of food delivery contribution. No plausible cannibalisation rate makes Bistro material today. What is material is that food delivery fell from 63% to 35% of group order value while Blinkit added roughly 900 stores, and that Blinkit's CEO now runs the parent company.

Slide 2 · Why this question, why now

Eternal already tried 10 minute food on the marketplace. It failed for a structural reason.

Bistro is the owned kitchen answer to that failure, launched into a market where three competitors were already live.

"We are actually shutting down both these initiatives… The current restaurant density and kitchen infrastructure is not set up for delivering orders in 10 minutes, which leads to inconsistent customer experience. As a result, we did not see any incrementality in demand while we ran Quick as an experiment for a few months." Deepinder Goyal on shutting Zomato Quick and Everyday. Q4FY25 shareholders' letter, Q15.

A marketplace does not control the kitchen. That is why Quick could not work and it is exactly what an owned kitchen changes. The competitive sequence matters too: Bistro followed, it did not lead.

DateEvent

May 2025Eternal shuts Zomato Quick and Everyday

Bistro entered Delhi NCR after both Bolt and Zepto Cafe were live there. Within eight weeks of its own launch Bolt was already 5% of Swiggy's food delivery orders. Management now frames Bistro against Toing and Ownly explicitly: "our answer to the question these platforms are pretending to solve… food delivery at INR 50-150 price points."

What this changes in the model. If a cannibalised order was heading to Bolt anyway, Eternal forfeits nothing by serving it from its own kitchen. That would raise the bar's slope in the company's favour. This deck does not take that credit: every figure assumes 100% retention, the most conservative case.
Slide 3 · The two business models

A cannibalised order is not a loss. It is an order that changed business model.

The same rupee of order value is worth different amounts depending on which model carries it.

Zomato · platform operator

  • Restaurants cook. Eternal takes a slice of the order.
  • Asset light. No inventory, no kitchen, no staff.
  • Contribution: 10.19% of NOV, disclosed every quarter.
  • Every order feeds restaurant selection, which is the moat.

Bistro · dealer

  • Eternal owns the kitchen and keeps the whole order value.
  • Pays food COGS, kitchen cost, delivery, packaging.
  • Contribution: not disclosed. No standalone P&L.
  • Contributes nothing to restaurant selection.

There is no arithmetic reason the two must differ, which is precisely why the threshold is worth computing rather than asserting. Zomato takes a small slice of a large order. Bistro takes all of a small one and pays to produce it.

Why CM_zomato is a read, not an assumption. Eternal publishes food delivery Contribution every quarter and publishes its definition: Adjusted Revenue less last mile delivery, platform funded discounts, payment gateway and COD handling, customer support and appeasement, refunds, and delivery partner onboarding. Every line moves with order volume. None of it is fixed cost, overhead, brand marketing or technology. That is already the marginal cost base a switching value needs, so no fixed cost add back is required.

Slide 4 · Who actually substitutes

Bistro and Zomato serve different willingness to pay. That caps how high cannibalisation can go.

This is the slide that defends the baseline. Two products at different price points are not one market with a gap. They are two segments, sorted by self selection.

SourceWhat management says Bistro is for
Q3FY25, at launchThe in office market for snacks, meals and beverages in 10 to 15 minutes, "currently addressed by on-site vendors and vending machines"
Q1FY26"Two demand pockets so far unaddressed by Zomato: customers looking for high quality but low cost meals (think customers who buy from home chefs), and customers looking for snacky food in 10 mins"
Q1FY27Food delivery at "INR 50-150 price points", which "can't be done without supply chain innovation… a different segment, a different occasion, and a genuinely new market"

A ₹50 to ₹150 ticket sits far below any plausible restaurant delivery order. For scale, Blinkit's net average order value is ₹518. And Eternal has already run the experiment that tests substitution directly: Quick offered 10 minute delivery from Zomato's own restaurants and produced no incrementality in demand. Restaurant supplied fast food did not expand the market. Bistro is a different bet on a different price point.

Gap, stated plainly. The overlap share between the two price distributions has not been measured. It needs a hand sampled menu comparison, which requires app access with a location and cannot be built from filings. Until it exists, this argument is directional and the deck runs the cannibalisation rate all the way to 100% rather than resting on a baseline.
Slide 5 · Unit economics and the switching value

The bar Bistro has to clear

Bistro's own margin cannot be estimated honestly from public data, so the analysis inverts the threshold and states the bar instead of guessing the number.

CM_net  =  CM_bistro − (α × retention × CM_zomato)
required CM_bistro  =  α × retention × CM_zomato

At α = 1 and retention = 1 the condition collapses to CM_bistro > CM_zomato. Under total cannibalisation the entire question is whether an owned kitchen earns more per rupee of order value than the marketplace does. Nothing else in the model matters at that point.

And the stake is small. Bistro sits inside an Others segment worth ₹95 crore a quarter, 0.88% of food delivery NOV, shared with Nugget and Greening India. The absolute worst case, total cannibalisation with zero Bistro margin, costs about ₹10 crore a quarter.

Slide 6 · What to monitor, and named risks

The contest that was actually settled

While the cannibalisation question was being debated, food delivery went from 63% to 35% of group order value and Blinkit's CEO became CEO of the parent company.

On 21 January 2026 the board accepted Deepinder Goyal's resignation as MD and CEO and appointed Albinder Singh Dhindsa, then CEO of Blinkit, as Chief Executive Officer of Eternal. The company had already renamed itself away from the food delivery brand in March 2025, and food delivery's COO resigned that April. Food delivery does not retain independent capital authority in any meaningful sense.

Three risks, with mitigants

RiskWhy it bitesMitigant
Bistro scales before the margin is proven The bar is only cheap while Bistro is small. At 10x the kitchens, a sub-10.2% margin becomes a real drag. Gate kitchen additions on disclosed Others segment contribution, not on order growth.
The order value framing is wrong α here is measured on order value, not orders, because food delivery Orders and AOV are not disclosed. If one Bistro order displaces one whole Zomato order, the bar shown is too low. Sample Bistro and Zomato menu prices. It is the single missing input and it closes this directly.
Bistro erodes the moat, not the margin Restaurant selection is the primary entry barrier. A Bistro order adds nothing to it, and a dealer typically supports lower volume and narrower selection than a platform operator. Track monthly active restaurant partners. It fell 344k to 328k in Q1FY27, the first material decline in a series that had otherwise risen every quarter since Q3FY23.
The gate. This is a conditional read, not a blank cheque. Revisit if Others segment revenue passes about 3% of food delivery NOV, or if food delivery contribution margin falls below 9.5% of NOV for two consecutive quarters. Both are disclosed quarterly, so the trigger is observable within 90 days of it firing.
What would change the answer, in order of impact. 1. A menu price sample, which closes the substitution question and the order versus value framing. 2. Bistro broken out of the Others segment, which would replace the entire threshold with a comparison. 3. City level food delivery NOV, which would allow a real difference in differences using Bistro's staged rollout. None of the three is public today.

What the observable data shows

Data integrity

Eternal's Annexure A tables are vector graphics with no text layer, so the panel was transcribed from rendered page images rather than parsed. Three controls: every quarter that appears in two overlapping letters matched exactly with no restatements; quick commerce NOV divided by Orders reproduces the separately disclosed net average order value within 2% in every quarter, which is asserted in the test suite; and any implausible quarter on quarter move is held by a validation gate until it carries a written explanation. One breach was raised and signed off.
